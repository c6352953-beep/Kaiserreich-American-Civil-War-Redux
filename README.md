# Kaiserreich: American Civil War Redux

A Kaiserreich submod project focused on adapting American Civil War content from Kaiserredux into Kaiserreich.

## Scope

The planned port covers American factions and breakaway states, political paths, national focus trees, events, decisions, national spirits, leaders and advisors, history files, localization, graphics, flags, portraits, and supporting mechanics.

## Compatibility

Initial target: Hearts of Iron IV 1.19.x and the corresponding current Kaiserreich release. Compatibility will be checked against the actual installed source files before implementation.

## Credits and permissions

Kaiserreich and Kaiserredux are separate projects. Their source code and assets remain the property of their respective contributors and are subject to their applicable licenses and contribution rules. No third-party code or assets should be redistributed until those terms have been verified. Keep attribution and required notices with any permitted reuse.
